Selected Projects

OMNICOOL — 1 MW-Class Two-Phase Data Center Cooling System (ARPA-E) Aug 2025 — Present

Developing system-level thermal-hydraulic models for a 1 MW-class two-phase direct-to-chip data center cooling system, with current work focused on a 250 kW unit. Work with industry partners, equipment vendors, and academic collaborators to integrate hardware information, equipment performance data, experimental measurements, and system requirements into physics-based Flow Network Models.

  • Lead thermal-fluid modeling and system integration activities, translating equipment specifications, performance curves, CAD information, operating requirements, and experimental data into component and system-level Flow Network Models.
  • Coordinate component development and model validation with vendors and project partners, incorporating engineering feedback, experimental measurements, and established correlations to refine models and investigate discrepancies.
  • Conduct thermal and hydraulic performance analyses and parametric studies to evaluate system behavior and support cooling-system design and integration decisions.
ES2 Project 5.1 — Single-Phase vs. Two-Phase Data Center Cooling

Developing end-to-end thermal-fluid models of single-phase and two-phase liquid-cooling architectures to evaluate thermal response, cooling performance, flow behavior, and system-level operating characteristics under varying heat loads.

Two-Phase Data Center Component Library — Flownex

Developed a dedicated two-phase data center cooling component library in the Flownex Simulation Environment, including cold plates, quick disconnects, flow regulators, and heat exchangers. Developed and validated component models using data from a highly instrumented two-phase rack at Villanova University, applying heat-transfer and pressure-drop correlations to characterize component and system performance.

Thermal-Fluid Experimental Test Bench

Designed and constructed a thermal-fluid experimental bench for prototyping, testing, and characterization of cold-plate geometries, including skived-fin, porous-foam, and gyroid structures. Performed flow-loop assembly, sensor installation and wiring, instrumentation, measurement-system setup, data acquisition, and troubleshooting to evaluate thermal and hydraulic performance and support model validation.

Physics-Based Digital Twin

Developed a physics-based Digital Twin architecture coupling thermal-fluid system simulation with telemetry, parametric controls, and 3D visualization. Integrated system simulation outputs with an interactive visualization environment for monitoring and analysis of complex thermal-fluid systems.
